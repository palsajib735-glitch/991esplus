Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Scientific Calculator handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Scientific Calculator is primarily designed to work offline. We do not knowingly collect or sell personal information such as your name, phone number, contacts, or location.

2. Calculator Data

Calculations, expressions, settings, and history may be stored locally on your device to provide app functionality. This information is not automatically sent to our servers.

3. Photos and OCR

If you use the photo-solving or OCR feature, the selected image is processed to recognize mathematical text. Offline OCR processing is performed on your device where supported. Images are not uploaded by the app during offline processing.

If you voluntarily enable an external AI or online solving service, the relevant image or text may be sent to that service. Please review the third party's privacy policy before using that feature.

4. Internet and Update Checks

The app may connect to the internet to check for application updates. These requests may be handled through public GitHub services. The app does not use this feature to collect personal information.

5. Third-Party Services

Some app features may use third-party libraries or services, such as offline text recognition or update services. These services may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the app and its locally stored information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app does not intentionally collect personal data, we do not knowingly maintain such information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
